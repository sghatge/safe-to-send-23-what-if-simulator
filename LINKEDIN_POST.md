Safe to Send · Ep 23 — The training that adds zero points

A team scores 59 on AI readiness. The manager books a prompt-engineering course for everyone. Next quarter: 59 again.

I built a what-if simulator to show why. For one synthetic team, it tries every single improvement and reports what each one does to the final score.

Prompt-engineering skills: +0
Practical AI skills: +0
Applying AI to real work: +0
…ten improvements, all +0.
Fixing data handling: +27.

The team was capped by one flag: people pasting customer data into tools they shouldn't. Until that's fixed, nothing else counts.

Run the same simulator on a team with no cap and the picture flips: every improvement is worth 1 to 3 points, and no single lever dominates.

Same model, different team, different strategy. That's why the what-if belongs next to the score.
